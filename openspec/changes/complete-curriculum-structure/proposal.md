## Why

The repository currently promises a more complete curriculum than it actually delivers. Learner-facing documents, course syllabi, case-study placement, and lab scaffolds diverge in ways that create confusion about what is implemented, what is optional, and what is still missing.

## What Changes

- Align the curriculum contract across the top-level guides, course syllabi, and per-course folders so the documented learning path matches the repository contents.
- Define and apply a canonical case-study organization model, including consistent course-to-case-study mapping and a shared quality bar for case-study structure.
- Complete the missing or placeholder lab surfaces needed to support the promised hands-on path, including experiment framing, validation steps, and starter/solution expectations.
- Close structural gaps in Course 01 and Course 02 where the current materials promise exercises or labs that are not currently present.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `curriculum-contract-alignment`: Ensure learner-facing curriculum documents, course syllabi, and course directories agree on scope, sequencing, prerequisites, and required work.
- `case-study-normalization`: Establish a canonical case-study model and require consistent formatting, ownership, and course linkage across the curriculum.
- `lab-experiment-completeness`: Require every promised lab to include an implementable scaffold, validation path, and concrete experiment-driven learning outcomes.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Affected areas include top-level curriculum documentation, per-course syllabi, course-local case-study folders, the global case-study library, lab README files, and lab starter/solution scaffolds across Courses 01 through 06.
